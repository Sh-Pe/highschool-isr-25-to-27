	\textbf{Question: }
	
	What life skills does this story teach us for achieving success?
	
	\textbf{Answer: }
	
	The story ``Rules of the Game'' teaches us many life skills for achieving success. One example is the dedication and endurance to keep working and doing what is needed to succeed, without giving up. Wavely spent most of her time learning and advancing in chess by reading books and studying how her opponents play. The hard work paid off and Wavely got notable achievements in chess. 
	
	The story teaches us other life skills, like communicating with our close associates how to help us when we need them, and vice versa. On one hand, Wavely has the time to spend on chess only as her mother freed her from housework such as doing the dishes, and offloaded the work to her brothers. On the other hand, occasions where her mother was invasive and caused her to lose focus resulted in a productivity loss and harmed her. Hence, we learn that it is important to have a supportive environment that not only wants, but also knows how to help us, therefore we should have the skill of communicating how to help us, as well as the skill of understanding how to help others, if we want to achieve success.
