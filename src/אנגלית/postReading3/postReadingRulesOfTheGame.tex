	\textbf{Question: }What life skills does this story teach us for achieving success?
	
	\textbf{Answer: }The story, ``Rules of the Game'', teach us many things about achieving success. One example, is the dedication and endurance to keep working and doing what you want to succeed in -- Wavely spent most of her waking hours on learning and advancing in chess by reading books and learning her opponents. The hard work paid of and Wavely got great achievements in chess. Another  things the story teach us is the importance of help from the environment. Wavely has the time to spend on Chess only because her mother freed her up from many houseworks and other jobs. Meanwhile, everything that her mother did that caused her to not focus, decreased significantly her ability to win games.
