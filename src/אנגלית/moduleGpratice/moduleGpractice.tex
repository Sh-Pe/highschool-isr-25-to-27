	\textbf{Question:}
	
	Some people believe that social media has improved communication, while others think it has made us more isolated. What is your opinion?
	
	\textbf{Answer: }
	
	I think social media has improved communication. Social media made the whole world more reachable for everyone. 
	
	First of all, social media allows us to cross boundaries and borders. One person in China can now talk with another person in Israel. For example, my brother flew to France a year ago. Via social media apps, like WhatsApp, my family and I were able to communicate with him, across great distances. 
	
	Secondly, social media enables us to bring many people into one place. Previously, if thousands of people had wanted to talk, it would have been merely impossible, or would have huge logistical problems. Nowadays, it is only a matter of posting a post on Instagram. As a result, prestigious research studies at Harvard have shown that 95\% of social media users feel more connected to the world compared to those who decide to not use social media. 
	
	In conclusion, I think it is crystal clear that social media improved our communications. Social provides the options to connect a large number of people, no matter the distance, which helps immensely to improve communication across the globe.
